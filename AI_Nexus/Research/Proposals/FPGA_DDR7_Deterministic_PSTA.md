# Research Proposal: FPGA/DDR7 Deterministic PSTA (The Lidar Lens)

## 🔬 1. The "Why"
To solve the "Hardware-Software Jitter" problem where the main OS/CPU/GPU introduces non-deterministic latency in safety-critical VSS calculations. By moving the "Lidar Lens" and PSTA Kernel to an FPGA on the DDR7 bus, we achieve nanosecond-level deterministic safety enforcement.

## 📐 2. SWOT Analysis: FPGA-Backed Determinism

### **Strengths (S)**
*   **Zero-Hitch Safety:** Deterministic execution independent of CPU load or OS interrupts.
*   **Parallel Reduction:** Massive Lidar point cloud processing at wire-speed.
*   **Asymmetric Security:** The FPGA (Safety) is physically isolated from the GPU (AI), preventing AI-level compromise from disabling safety triggers.

### **Weaknesses (W)**
*   **High Complexity:** Requires VHDL/Verilog expertise and low-level memory controller interfacing.
*   **Rigid Architecture:** Harder to iterate on the math once burned into bitstreams compared to Python/C++.
*   **Development Barrier:** Testing requires specialized hardware (Dev Boards) and PCIe-DMA integration.

### **Opportunities (O)**
*   **The "Marine Bottle" Edge:** Enables high-fidelity safety on low-power autonomous vessels without requiring the 5090's full power for safety.
*   **Deterministic Forensics:** Logs generated by the FPGA are "Physical Truth" markers that cannot be spoofed by a compromised AI.

### **Threats (T)**
*   **Memory Bus Contention:** DDR7 bandwidth is high, but the FPGA must not starve the GPU's inference engine.
*   **Hardware Fragmentation:** Specific FPGA implementations may not scale across different Marine Bottle versions (V1-V4).
